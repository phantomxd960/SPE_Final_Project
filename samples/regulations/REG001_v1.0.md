# REG001 Transaction Reporting and Record-Keeping Regulation

Version 1.0 · Effective 2025-01-01 · Issued by the Financial Conduct Board (fictional authority, demonstration data)

## 1. Scope and Definitions

1.1 This regulation applies to every credit institution and payment institution authorised in the jurisdiction.

1.2 A cash transaction is any deposit, withdrawal or exchange of banknotes or coins, whether carried out in one operation or in several operations that appear to be linked.

## 2. Customer Due Diligence

2.1 Institutions must verify the identity of each customer before opening an account or carrying out an occasional transaction.

2.2 Institutions must identify every beneficial owner who holds 25% or more of a corporate customer.

## 3. Ongoing Monitoring

3.1 Institutions must monitor customer transactions on an ongoing basis to detect activity that is unusual for the customer profile.

3.2 Monitoring rules are reviewed by the compliance function at least once every 12 months.

## 4. Transaction Reporting

4.1 Institutions must appoint a reporting officer who is responsible for all reports made under this regulation.

<!-- page 3 -->

4.2.1 Institutions must report every cash transaction of EUR 10,000 or more to the Financial Intelligence Unit within 10 business days.

4.2.2 Reports should be submitted electronically through the authority's reporting portal.

4.3 Institutions must not inform the customer that a report has been made.

## 5. Record Keeping

5.1 Records of reports and supporting documents must be kept for 5 years after the transaction.

5.2 Records must be made available to the authority on request.

<!-- page 4 -->

## 6. Training

6.1 Staff who handle cash transactions should receive training on reporting obligations every year.
