# Transaction Monitoring Policy

Regulation: REG001
Process: Transaction Monitoring
Control: CTRL-TM-01 Daily large-cash alert

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This policy describes how the bank detects cash transactions that must be reported to the authority.

## 2. Alerts

2.1 The monitoring system raises an alert for every cash transaction of EUR 10,000 or more, as required by clause 4.2.1 of REG001.

2.2 An analyst reviews each alert within 2 business days and forwards confirmed cases to the reporting officer.

## 3. Rule Maintenance

3.1 Alert thresholds and scenarios are recalibrated whenever the underlying regulation changes.
