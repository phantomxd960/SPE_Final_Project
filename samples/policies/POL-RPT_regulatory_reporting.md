# Regulatory Reporting Policy

Regulation: REG001
Process: Regulatory Reporting
Control: CTRL-RPT-02 Report submission check

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This policy sets out how the Reporting Unit prepares and files regulatory reports.

## 2. Filing

2.1 Reports required under REG001 clause 4.2.1 are filed by the Reporting Unit within 10 business days of the transaction.

2.2 Reports are submitted through the reporting portal in the format described in clause 4.2.2.

2.3 A second reviewer checks every report before submission.
