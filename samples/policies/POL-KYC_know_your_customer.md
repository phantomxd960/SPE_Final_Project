# Know Your Customer Policy

Regulation: REG001
Process: Customer Onboarding

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This policy explains how the bank establishes and verifies the identity of its customers.

## 2. Identification

2.1 The identity of every customer is verified with an official document before an account is opened, in line with clause 2.1.

2.2 Beneficial owners of corporate customers are identified as described in clause 2.2.
