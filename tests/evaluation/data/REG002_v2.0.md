# REG002 Consumer Credit Disclosure Regulation

Version 2.0 · Effective 2026-07-01 · Issued by the Financial Conduct Board (fictional authority, evaluation data)

## 1. Scope

1.1 This regulation applies to lenders offering consumer credit agreements to individuals.

1.2 Any arrangement in which a lender extends credit to a private person for personal use is a consumer credit agreement.

## 2. Pre-contract Disclosure

2.1 Lenders must disclose the annual percentage rate of charge before the agreement is signed.

2.2 The annual percentage rate must not exceed 30% for unsecured personal loans.

2.3 Lenders must provide a summary of the key terms in plain language.

2.4 Lenders must disclose the total amount payable by the borrower before the agreement is signed.

## 3. Right of Withdrawal

3.1 Borrowers may cancel the agreement within 14 calendar days and do not need to give any reason for doing so.

3.2 Lenders must refund any fees within 30 days of a withdrawal.

## 4. Creditworthiness

4.1 Lenders must assess the creditworthiness of the borrower before granting credit.

4.2 The assessment must consider the income and the existing debts of the borrower.

## 5. Statements

5.1 Lenders must offer a free repayment plan to borrowers who report financial difficulty.

5.2 Lenders must send a statement to the borrower every 3 months.

5.3 Statements must show the outstanding balance and the interest charged in the period.

## 6. Fees and Complaints

6.1 Lenders may charge a late payment fee of up to EUR 15.

## 7. Entry into Force

7.1 This regulation enters into force on 1 July 2026.
