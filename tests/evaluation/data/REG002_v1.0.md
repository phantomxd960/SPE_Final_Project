# REG002 Consumer Credit Disclosure Regulation

Version 1.0 · Effective 2025-01-01 · Issued by the Financial Conduct Board (fictional authority, evaluation data)

## 1. Scope

1.1 This regulation applies to lenders offering consumer credit agreements to individuals.

1.2 A consumer credit agreement is any agreement under which a lender grants credit to an individual for personal purposes.

## 2. Pre-contract Disclosure

2.1 Lenders must disclose the annual percentage rate of charge before the agreement is signed.

2.2 The annual percentage rate must not exceed 36% for unsecured personal loans.

2.3 Lenders should provide a summary of the key terms in plain language.

## 3. Right of Withdrawal

3.1 Borrowers may withdraw from the agreement within 14 calendar days without giving a reason.

3.2 Lenders must refund any fees within 30 days of a withdrawal.

## 4. Creditworthiness

4.1 Lenders must assess the creditworthiness of the borrower before granting credit.

4.2 The assessment must consider the income and the existing debts of the borrower.

## 5. Statements

5.1 Lenders must send a statement to the borrower every 3 months.

5.2 Statements must show the outstanding balance and the interest charged in the period.

## 6. Fees and Complaints

6.1 Lenders may charge a late payment fee of up to EUR 25.

6.2 Complaints must be acknowledged within 5 business days.

## 7. Entry into Force

7.1 This regulation enters into force on 1 January 2025.
